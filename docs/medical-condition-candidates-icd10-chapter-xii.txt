# The chapter centre is carrier strength: atopic dermatitis, eczema,
# psoriasis, urticaria, rosacea, acne, vitiligo, alopecia areata, melanoma and
# the non-melanoma skin cancers arrived from the carrier and PTSD-phenome
# sources long ago, and the 1.15.0 spelling-variant work and the boundary
# probes already watch rash, hives and mole. The sweep's job here is the same
# one every chapter posed: find the named diagnoses the centre left silent —
# the bullous and papulosquamous rarities, the erythemas, the radiation rows,
# the follicular and sweat disorders, the chronic-wound and autoimmune-skin
# residuals.
#
# ── Group A: in-diseases-classified-elsewhere routing. A title whose place
# tells the coder the condition belongs to another chapter, not something a
# person reports — the person names the underlying disease or the finding,
# which is watched or mapped on its own row. Declared here: L14 Bullous
# disorders in diseases classified elsewhere; L45 Papulosquamous disorders in
# diseases classified elsewhere; L54 Erythema in diseases classified
# elsewhere; L62 Nail disorders in diseases classified elsewhere; L86
# Keratoderma in diseases classified elsewhere; and L99 Other disorders of
# skin and subcutaneous tissue in diseases classified elsewhere.
#
# ── Group B: postprocedural constructs. A postprocedural disorder is coded
# from the procedure, and is not a statement a visitor makes. Declared here:
# L76 Intraoperative and postprocedural complications of skin and
# subcutaneous tissue.
#
