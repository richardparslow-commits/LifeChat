#
# The chapter is the digestive system — carrier-questionnaire strength in its
# centre (IBS, GERD/heartburn, gastritis, cirrhosis, pancreatitis, Crohn's,
# colitis, diverticular disease, gallstones/gallbladder, hernia, hemorrhoids
# and piles all arrived from the carrier and critical-illness sources, and the
# 2026-09-18 boundary probes carried dental caries and gingivitis) and blind
# spots of the familiar kind at its edges: the dental and salivary rows, the
# appendiceal rows, the peritoneal and hepatic-failure rows, the
# cholecystitis/biliary rows and the intestinal-obstruction rows never
# appeared on a questionnaire, and this sweep measures whether they can stay
# absent from a vocabulary that claims medical-history coverage.
#
# The 7 titles that are not candidates are declared here rather than dropped,
# because "this row cannot be a disclosure" is a finding the next sweep should
# re-read rather than rediscover. Each block names the row it covers and the
# reason, and each covered row's *stateable* form — the sentence a person
# would actually write — is watched by a gate term or mapped as a canonical
# condition, which is why the declarations are safe: the chapter's content is
# covered, its navigation is not pretended to be a statement.
#
# ── Group A: in-diseases-classified-elsewhere routing. A title whose place
# tells the coder the condition belongs to another chapter, not something a
# person reports — the person names the underlying disease or the organ
# finding, which is watched or mapped on its own row. Declared here: K23
# Disorders of esophagus in diseases classified elsewhere; K67 Disorders of
# peritoneum in infectious diseases classified elsewhere; K77 Liver disorders
# in diseases classified elsewhere; and K87 Disorders of gallbladder, biliary
# tract and pancreas in diseases classified elsewhere.
#
# ── Group B: postprocedural constructs. A postprocedural disorder is coded
# from the procedure, and is not a statement a visitor makes. Declared here:
# K91 Intraoperative and postprocedural complications of the digestive
# system; K94 Complications of artificial openings of the digestive tract
# (the stoma state itself is watched, not this complication coding); and K95
# Complications of bariatric procedures.
#
# Measure it with:
#   npm run conditions:sweep -- docs/medical-condition-candidates-icd10-chapter-xi.txt
