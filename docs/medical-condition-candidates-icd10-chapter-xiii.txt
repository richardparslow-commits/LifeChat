# The chapter centre is carrier strength: gout, rheumatoid arthritis, SLE,
# psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, scleroderma, fibromyalgia,
# osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, osteopenia, scoliosis, intervertebral disc
# disorders, spinal stenosis, osteomyelitis, sciatica, Ehlers-Danlos, Marfan,
# carpal tunnel, tennis elbow, trigger finger, bursitis and ganglion cyst all
# arrived from the carrier and PTSD-phenome sources long ago. The sweep's job
# here is the named edges — the arthropathy residuals, the joint derangements
# and TMJ, the vasculitides and myositides, Sjögren's and polymyalgia
# rheumatica, the dorsopathy family, spondylosis and cervical discs, low back
# pain, the tendon and shoulder lesions, Dupuytren's and plantar fasciitis,
# frozen shoulder and rotator cuff, osteomalacia, osteonecrosis, Paget's,
# stress fractures and the osteochondroses.
#
# ── Group A: in-diseases-classified-elsewhere routing. A title whose place
# tells the coder the condition belongs to another chapter, not something a
# person reports — the person names the underlying disease, which is watched
# or mapped on its own row. Declared here: M01 Direct infections of joint in
# infectious and parasitic diseases classified elsewhere; M14 Arthropathies
# in other diseases classified elsewhere; M36 Systemic disorders of
# connective tissue in other diseases classified elsewhere; M49
# Spondylopathies in diseases classified elsewhere; M63 Disorders of muscle
# in diseases classified elsewhere; and M90 Osteopathies in diseases
# classified elsewhere.
#
# ── Group B: postprocedural constructs. A postprocedural disorder is coded
# from the procedure, and is not a statement a visitor makes. Declared here:
# M96 Intraoperative and postprocedural complications and disorders of
# musculoskeletal system, not elsewhere classified; and M97 Periprosthetic
# fracture around internal prosthetic joint.
#
