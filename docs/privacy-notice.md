# Privacy Notice — Life Policy Pilot

**Effective date:** 2026-08-30 · **Version:** 1.0.0
**Controller:** Richard Parslow / Life Policy Pilot · Life Policy Pilot Blog · Contact: privacy@lifepolicypilot.blog

This Privacy Notice describes how Life Policy Pilot ("we," "us") processes personal data collected
through the website (including the Life Policy Pilot AI Educational Assistant chat) and how Texas
consumers can exercise their rights under the **Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA)**,
effective July 1, 2024.

> Compliance note: this is the TDPSA-compliant notice for the blog's privacy page. It must be
> reviewed by Texas insurance counsel before publication, and it is linked from the chat disclosure
> and consent flows (`PRIVACY_NOTICE_URL`). Any material change requires an updated version recorded
> in the compliance matrix.

---

## 1. Categories of personal data we process

- **Identity and contact data** — name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact channel,
  provided when you ask for a follow-up or appointment.
- **Profile/preference data** — optional qualification answers (goal category, decision timeline,
  whether you currently have coverage), product interest, and coverage-amount interest.
- **Medical data (only with your explicit, separate consent)** — date of birth, gender, height/weight,
  tobacco or nicotine use, diagnosed conditions, and prescribed medications, used solely to match
  carriers to your profile. This is **sensitive data** under the TDPSA and is never collected without
  your affirmative, versioned consent.
- **Chat and usage data** — the text you send in the chat, session identifiers, the page you were
  viewing, and technical analytics (no PII in analytics events).

## 2. Purposes of processing

- Providing general educational life-insurance information and answering your questions.
- Facilitating a follow-up conversation or appointment with Richard Parslow, a licensed Texas
  life-insurance broker, when you request it.
- Matching carriers to your profile when you give separate medical consent.
- Responding to data subject requests and consumer inquiries.
- Improving the site and assistant through de-identified analytics (categorical events only).

## 3. Categories of data shared with third parties

- **Contact and profile data** — shared with Richard Parslow (and, when you book an appointment, the
  scheduling provider) for the follow-up you requested.
- **Medical data** — shared only with Richard Parslow for carrier matching, and only with your
  affirmative consent.
- **De-identified analytics** — shared with analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics/GTM) as
  categorical, non-PII events.
- We do **not sell** personal data, and we do not share personal data for targeted advertising.

## 4. Categories of third parties

- **Richard Parslow** (licensed Texas life-insurance broker) — the recipient of contact and consented
  medical data for follow-up and carrier matching.
- **Analytics providers** — receive only de-identified, categorical event data.
- **Scheduling/calendar providers** — when you book an appointment, receive the contact and scheduling
  details needed to arrange it.
- **Service processors** — hosting, LLM/chat infrastructure, and CRM providers bound by processor
  contracts (instructions, security, rights-assistance, subprocessor governance, incident notice,
  return/delete, and training-use disclosure).

## 5. How to exercise your rights (TDPSA consumer rights)

You may request to **access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy** of your personal data, or
**withdraw consent** at any time:

- **Email:** privacy@lifepolicypilot.blog
- **Via the assistant:** the chat disclosure directs you here; the chat itself is not the place to
  share sensitive data needed to verify a request.

We will respond to a verified request within **45 days** (extendable by 45 days with notice). We may
ask you to verify your identity before acting. You may appeal a refusal, and you may contact the
**Texas Attorney General** if you believe your rights were violated.

We also honor opt-out signals to the extent required by law, maintain a **Do-Not-Call** suppression
list for marketing contacts, and treat **"Reply STOP"** to any text as a withdrawal of text consent.

## 6. Retention and security

- Personal data is retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, per our
  counsel-approved retention schedule, then deleted unless a legal hold applies.
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256), access is role-based with MFA, and
  conversations are **not** used for model training without separate, verified consent.
- You may delete your session data at any time by emailing the DSR contact above.

## 7. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our practices change and record the version in the compliance
matrix. Material changes require counsel review before taking effect.
