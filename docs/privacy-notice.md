## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Notice describes how **Richard Parslow, an independent life insurance broker licensed
in the State of Texas (Texas License #3090432)**, collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal
information when you:

- Visit the Life Policy Pilot blog (lifepolicypilot.blog)
- Interact with the LifeChat AI Educational Assistant
- Submit a request for a consultation or appointment
- Communicate with us by email, phone, or other means

Richard Parslow is an **independent broker, not an insurance company, agency, or carrier**. When you
purchase a policy through Richard Parslow, the insurance carrier that issues your policy will have
its own privacy notice that governs how it handles your information.

## 2. What This Notice Covers

Two laws protect your information, and this notice covers both:

- **GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act)** — covers "nonpublic personal information" (NPI): information
  that identifies you — like your name, address, phone number, email, health information, or
  financial information — that is not publicly available. GLBA expects a clear notice about what we
  collect, how we use and share it, and reasonable safeguards to protect it.
- **TDPSA (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act)** — gives Texas consumers rights over their personal
  data, including the right to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy. Health
  information is "sensitive data" under TDPSA and is only collected with your explicit consent.

This notice covers the blog, the LifeChat assistant, consultation and appointment requests, and
email, phone, or other communications with us.

## 3. Information We Collect

- **Identity and contact information** — your name, email address, phone number, and preferred
  contact channel, provided when you ask for a follow-up or appointment.
- **Profile and preference information** — optional qualification answers (your goal, your decision
  timeline, whether you currently have coverage), product interest, and coverage-amount interest.
- **Health information (sensitive)** — date of birth, gender, height/weight, tobacco or nicotine
  use, diagnosed conditions, and prescribed medications. Collected **only** with your explicit,
  separate, versioned consent, and used solely to match carriers to your profile.
- **Chat and usage information** — the text you send in the chat, session identifiers, the page you
  were viewing, and de-identified analytics events (no personal information in analytics).

## 4. How We Use Your Information

- To answer your general life-insurance education questions.
- To prepare for and follow up on a consultation or appointment you request with Richard Parslow.
- To match carriers to your profile, when you give separate consent for health information.
- To carry out your insurance request — such as an application, policy service, or claim — with the
  carrier you choose.
- To respond to your privacy and data-subject requests.
- To improve the site and assistant through de-identified analytics.
- To meet legal obligations (for example, fraud prevention, regulatory, and tax requirements).

## 5. Information We Share

GLBA allows sharing needed to provide the insurance service you asked for and with companies that
work for us. We share your information only:

- **With the carrier you choose** — the information needed to process your application, policy, or
  request. That carrier's own privacy notice then governs its handling.
- **With service providers** — companies we hire to help run the site and the assistant (hosting,
  chat/AI infrastructure, analytics, and scheduling). They are bound by contracts that require them
  to protect your information, follow our instructions, and help us respond to your requests.
- **As required by law** — with regulators (such as the Texas Department of Insurance), courts, or
  law enforcement when legally required.
- **With your consent or at your direction** — for example, when you submit a data-subject request.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not share it for marketing. **Your right to
opt out:** you can ask us at any time not to share your information for marketing purposes (we do
not do this today) by emailing privacy@lifepolicypilot.blog.

## 6. Your Rights (TDPSA Consumer Rights)

You may request to **access, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy** of your personal data, or
**withdraw consent**, at any time:

- **Email:** privacy@lifepolicypilot.blog
- **Via the assistant:** the chat disclosure directs you here; the chat itself is not the place to
  share sensitive data needed to verify a request.

We will respond to a verified request within **45 days** (extendable by 45 days with notice). We may
ask you to verify your identity before acting. You may appeal a refusal, and you may contact the
**Texas Attorney General** if you believe your rights were violated.

We also honor opt-out signals to the extent required by law, maintain a **Do-Not-Call** suppression
list for marketing contacts, and treat **"Reply STOP"** to any text as a withdrawal of text consent.

## 7. How We Protect Your Information (GLBA Safeguards)

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including:

- **Encryption** — information is encrypted while being sent to us (in transit) and while stored
  (at rest).
- **Access controls** — only people who need it can see it; access is limited and requires strong
  authentication (multi-factor where supported).
- **Trained staff and vendors** — the broker and any service providers are required to protect your
  information and to tell us if something goes wrong.
- **Data minimization** — we collect only what we need and keep it only as long as needed.

## 8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, per our
counsel-approved retention schedule, and then delete it unless a legal hold applies. You can request
deletion at any time through the contact below.

## 9. Children

The blog and the LifeChat assistant are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.
