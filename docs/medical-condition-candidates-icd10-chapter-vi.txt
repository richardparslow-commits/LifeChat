# ICD-10-CM Chapter VI (G00–G99), the three-character category titles of the
# whole chapter, verbatim FY 2026 — the eighth coverage-sweep corpus and the
# sixth codebook enumeration. Written down from the codebook enumeration before
# any Chapter VI row was mapped (2026-09-20): WHO lists 67 categories in the
# chapter and ICD-10-CM adds G14 (postpolio syndrome) while leaving G42
# unused, so the FY 2026 chapter carries 68 three-character titles, of which 50
# are candidates here.
#
# The chapter is the nervous system — the vocabulary's oldest strength (MS,
# migraine, epilepsy, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, ALS, myasthenia gravis all
# arrived from the carrier sources) and its largest blind spot of the same
# kind: the peripheral-nerve and muscle rows (trigeminal neuralgia, dystonia,
# Huntington's, Guillain-Barré, syringomyelia) never appeared on a carrier
# questionnaire, and this sweep measures whether they can stay absent from a
# vocabulary that claims medical-history coverage.
#
# The 18 titles that are not candidates are declared here rather than dropped,
# because "this row cannot be a disclosure" is a finding the next sweep should
# re-read rather than rediscover. Each block names the rows it covers and the
# reason, and each covered row's *stateable* form — the sentence a person would
# actually write — is watched by a gate term or mapped as a canonical
# condition, which is why the declarations are safe: the chapter's content is
# covered, its navigation is not pretended to be a statement.
#
# ── Group A: in-diseases-classified-elsewhere routing. A title whose place
# tells the coder the condition belongs to another chapter, not something a
# person reports — the person names the underlying disease, which is watched or
# mapped on its own row. Declared here: Meningitis in bacterial diseases
# classified elsewhere and Meningitis in other infectious and parasitic
# diseases classified elsewhere (the stateable form is "meningitis", carried at
# G03.9); Encephalitis, myelitis and encephalomyelitis in diseases classified
# elsewhere (the stateable form is "encephalitis"); Intracranial and intraspinal
# abscess and granuloma in diseases classified elsewhere; Parkinsonism in
# diseases classified elsewhere (the stateable form is parkinsonism, watched on
# the secondary-parkinsonism row); Extrapyramidal and movement disorders in
# diseases classified elsewhere; Other degenerative disorders of nervous system
# in diseases classified elsewhere; Vascular syndromes of brain in
# cerebrovascular diseases (the stateable form is "stroke", carried since the
# critical-illness source); Cranial nerve disorders, nerve root and plexus
# compressions, mononeuropathy, and polyneuropathy in diseases classified
# elsewhere (polyneuropathy is carried at G62.9); Disorders of myoneural
# junction and muscle in diseases classified elsewhere; Other disorders of brain
# in diseases classified elsewhere; Other disorders of nervous system in
# diseases classified elsewhere; and Systemic atrophies primarily affecting
# central nervous system in diseases classified elsewhere (the paraneoplastic
# constructs file under the cancer the person names).
#
# ── Group B: sequelae and postprocedural constructs. A sequela is coded from
# the original condition's own chapter; a postprocedural disorder is coded from
# the procedure, and neither is a statement a visitor makes. Declared here:
# Sequelae of inflammatory diseases of central nervous system; Postprocedural
# disorders of nervous system, not elsewhere classified.
#
